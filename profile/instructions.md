# Wall-garden, Build instructions, 2026 edition

- Prepare the wall. Plaster it with waterproof cement.

![wall-garden](../pictures/wall_preparation3.jpeg)

- Cut the bars to required size and angle. You may need some special bars to connect to roof.

![wall-garden](../pictures/cut_bars.jpeg)

- Connect the support to roof.

![wall-garden](../pictures/bars_to_roof.jpeg)

- Install a hydroisolation membrane to the wall.

![wall-garden](../pictures/wall_hydro-isolation.jpeg)

- Prepare the welded mesh. Must bend twice (at 90 degrees) on each lateral side so it also touches the wall. This will support the soil.

![wall-garden](../pictures/welded_mesh.jpeg)

- Install the plexiglass. I connected it with some strings to the mesh so it does not fall.

![wall-garden](../pictures/plexiglass_install.jpeg)

- Side view of structure

![wall-garden](../pictures/structure_side_view.jpeg)

- Fill with soil from the top.

![wall-garden](../pictures/soil_fill.jpeg)

- Dig holes in plexiglass. There are 3 steps here:
1. cut some horizontal lines with a 1mm grinder.
2. heat the plexiglass with a heat gun.
3. insert a conic tube to shape the holes.

![wall-garden](../pictures/holes_tool.jpeg)

- After holes are made:

![wall-garden](../pictures/holes.jpeg)

- Plant peppers.

![wall-garden](../pictures/after_planting_1.jpeg)

![wall-garden](../pictures/after_planting_2.jpeg)

- Cover them during sunny days. This is important if the Sun falls directly on them.

![wall-garden](../pictures/sun_cover_2.jpeg)

- What you finally get:

![wall-garden](../pictures/crops_5.jpeg)
